LT-CAES repository description
------------------------------

LT-CAES: screening simulator for low-temperature adiabatic compressed-air
energy storage. One plant, two uses of the stored heat - all of it back into
the turbines (LTA), or the top of the store sold as district heat (LTAHP).
Real-fluid thermodynamics and honest feasibility limits; no cost model and no
datasheets. (326 characters)

No-combustion archive description
---------------------------------

No-combustion CAES: the frozen three-concept screener. Ambient-diabatic AD
together with LTA and LTAHP, all fuel-free, compared on one normalized kilogram
of air. Kept for the diabatic baseline the active line does not simulate; the
low-temperature adiabatic family lives on main. (245 characters)
