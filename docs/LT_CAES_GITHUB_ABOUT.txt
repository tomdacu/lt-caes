No-combustion CAES repository description
-----------------------------------------

No-combustion CAES: a screening simulator that compares three fuel-free
compressed-air energy storage concepts - ambient-diabatic AD, low-temperature
adiabatic LTA, and its heat-and-power sibling LTAHP - on one normalized
kilogram of air. (229 characters, the one actually on GitHub)

LT-CAES branch description
--------------------------

LT-CAES: the low-temperature adiabatic family alone (LTA and LTAHP), cut from
main onto its own branch. One plant, two uses of the stored heat - back into
the turbines (LTA), or sold as district heat (LTAHP); the diabatic concept and
the mode axis are removed from that line. (279 characters)
