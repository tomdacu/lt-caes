CAES Atlas repository description
---------------------------------

CAES Atlas: thermodynamic screening of compressed-air energy storage without
combustion. Ambient-diabatic, low-temperature adiabatic and heat-and-power
plants on one real-fluid, per-kg-air basis, with closed exergy books and a map
of the constraint that binds. (260 characters)

LT-CAES branch description
--------------------------

LT-CAES: the low-temperature adiabatic family alone (LTA and LTAHP), cut from
main onto its own branch. One plant, two uses of the stored heat - back into
the turbines (LTA), or sold to a heat user (LTAHP); the diabatic concept and
the mode axis are removed from that line. (273 characters)
