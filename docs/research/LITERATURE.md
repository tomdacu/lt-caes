# LTA-CAES literature

## Core low-temperature A-CAES references

| Source | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| Daniel Wolf and Marcus Budt, 2014, *LTA-CAES - A low-temperature approach to Adiabatic Compressed Air Energy Storage*, Applied Energy 125, 158-164. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2014.03.013) | Foundational LTA-CAES layout: multi-stage compression/expansion, liquid two-tank TES, 90-200 °C target range, and water as the preferred low-cost medium. |
| Vittorio Tola, Francesca Carolina Marcello, Daniele Cocco, and Giorgio Cau, 2022, *Performance Assessment of Low-Temperature A-CAES Plants*, Journal of Thermal Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11630-022-1632-7) | Compares low-temperature configurations and their TES integration; useful for benchmarking heat-storage layouts and reported RTE. |
| Francesca Carolina Marcello, Davide Micheletto, Daniele Cocco, and Vittorio Tola, 2023, *Modelling and performance analysis of a Low Temperature A-CAES system coupled with renewable energy power plants*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.52202/069564-0187) | Connects LTA-CAES sizing and off-design operation to PV/wind integration and demand coverage. |
| Markus Hadam, 2021, *Thermodynamic analysis of a modular A-CAES with reversible turbo and piston engines*, doctoral dissertation, Ruhr University Bochum. | Relevant to modular LTA-CAES machinery and system-design choices. Search the university repository by title. |
| Eva Schischke, Annedore Kanngießer, Markus Hadam, and Marcus Budt, 2019, *Techno-economic Evaluation of a Modular Compressed Air Energy Storage to Support Integration of Wind Generation on El Hierro*. [Conference record](https://www.atlantis-press.com/proceedings/ires-19/125938253) | Useful for moving from component-level thermodynamics toward dispatch and techno-economic comparison. |

| Source | Why it matters here |
|---|---|

| Philipp Roos and Andreas Haselbacher, 2022, *Analytical modeling of advanced adiabatic compressed air energy storage: Literature review and new models*. [ETH record](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/handle/20.500.11850/547543?show=full) | Valuable for understanding assumptions in analytical A-CAES models and their limitations. |
